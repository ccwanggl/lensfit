# Double-Slit Fringe Spacing

For a double-slit experiment with slit separation `d`, wavelength `λ`, and screen distance `L`, the fringe spacing on the screen is:

$$
\Delta y = \frac{\lambda L}{d}
$$

## Variables

- `Δy` — fringe spacing on the screen
- `λ` — wavelength of light
- `L` — distance from slits to screen
- `d` — center-to-center slit separation
