# Interference

Interference occurs when two or more coherent light waves overlap. The resulting intensity is not the sum of individual intensities but depends on the phase difference between the waves.

## Key concepts

- **Constructive interference**: waves in phase add, producing bright regions.
- **Destructive interference**: waves 180° out of phase cancel, producing dark regions.
- **Coherence**: stable phase relationship is required for observable interference.
- **Path difference**: `ΔL = m λ` gives constructive interference; `ΔL = (m + 1/2) λ` gives destructive interference.
